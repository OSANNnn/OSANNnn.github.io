Game programmer

Programmer, gamer, and always learning.

After years leading teams in restaurant management, I went back to school while working full time, earned my IT diploma with top marks, and went straight into a game programming course.

I enjoy game programming in all its forms, from gameplay and UI to physics, but computer graphics is where I’m heading: how a frame comes together, what makes it fast, and what makes it look right. I’ve built with C++, OpenGL and Unreal Engine 5, plus some Unity and Godot on my own, and I learn by building, studying the best resources out there, and taking my own code apart to make it better.

Away from the code I’m usually playing something, or staring at a screenshot wondering how they lit that scene. I love art and game design as much as programming, because good code is only half of a good game.
